Official course description: A first course in modeling, characterization, and application of semiconductor devices and integrated circuits. Development of appropriate models for circuit-level behavior of diodes, bi-polar and field effect transistors, and non-ideal op-amps. Application in analysis and design of linear amplifiers. Frequency domain characterization of transistor circuits.

 

Requisites: ECE 203 and 230, or a member of Engineering Guest Students

 

Learning outcomes
Course Learning Outcomes: Students will be able to

Analyze functions and DC and AC operations of diodes, metal-oxide-semiconductor field effect transistors, and bipolar junction transistors
Utilize large and small signal models of diodes, metal-oxide-semiconductor field effect transistors and bipolar junction transistors in analysis of analog circuits
Analyze and design single stage analog amplifiers
Analyze and design basic differential amplifiers and multistage amplifiers
Analyze basic operational amplifier circuits
